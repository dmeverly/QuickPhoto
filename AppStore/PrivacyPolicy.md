# QuickPhoto Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 15, 2026_

QuickPhoto does not collect, store, or share any personal information.

- **Photos.** Photos you add are copied into QuickPhoto's private storage on your device. They are never uploaded or sent anywhere. QuickPhoto only sees the photos you choose; it cannot access the rest of your photo library.
- **Settings.** Your preferences (time per photo, grayscale, sounds) are saved on your device.
- **No accounts, analytics, advertising, or tracking.** QuickPhoto has no servers and makes no network connections.

Deleting QuickPhoto removes all of its photos and settings from your device.
