## Description (4000)
QuickPhoto is a simple timer for gesture drawing and figure sketching practice, using reference photos you choose.

Add photos from your library, choose how long each one stays on screen, and press play. When time runs out, QuickPhoto shows the next photo and restarts the countdown. Photos are shuffled so you see every one before any repeats.

FEATURES
• Use your own reference photos
• Time per photo: 30 seconds, 1, 2, 5 or 10 minutes, or any custom time
• Shuffled order with no repeats until you've seen them all
• Grayscale mode to focus on values and shapes
• Optional sound cues when a new photo starts and when time is almost up
• Pause, resume, or skip to the next photo at any time
• Keeps the screen awake while you draw

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your photos stay on your device. QuickPhoto has no accounts, no ads, no tracking, and never connects to the internet.
